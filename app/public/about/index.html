Read PDFs in the dark, with their colors.

Reader343 turns the pages of a PDF dark and leaves photos, charts and colored text as they are. Mark what matters, listen to the book, and find anything again. Your books and notes stay on your device.

Open the app Other ways to get it

Free and open source. No account.

Smart dark

Dark pages that keep what the author drew

Most dark modes turn a photo into its negative and a red warning into cyan. Reader343 darkens the paper and the text and leaves the rest alone.

  • Five dark themes and a sepia page
  • Brightness, contrast, sepia and grayscale, each on a slider
  • Scanned pages are darkened too; photos are not
  • Crop the margins, rotate, or read two pages side by side
Marks and notes

Highlight, underline, write in the margin

Select text and pick a color. Turn a highlight into an underline or a strikethrough, write a note on a passage, or drop a sticky note anywhere on the page.

  • Everything you marked in one panel, beside the page
  • A notebook for all your books, exported as Markdown, a spreadsheet or Anki cards
  • Draw with a pen, fill in forms and sign them
  • Share a passage as a picture, with the book and the page
  • Undo with Ctrl+Z or Cmd+Z
Read aloud

Let the book read itself to you

On a computer, natural voices read the text and the page follows along. They run on your machine; the text is not sent anywhere. On an iPhone the best voice of the device is used.

  • 28 natural English voices, after one download of 102 MB
  • Skips page numbers, running headers and footnote marks
  • Speed in steps of 0.25, a sleep timer, and auto-scroll for silent reading
Your library

Pick up where you stopped

The library opens with the books you are reading, each at its page. Tags and a star keep the rest in order, and one search looks through titles, your notes and the text of every book.

  • Search inside the book, with every match in a list
  • Contents, page thumbnails and links that show where they lead
  • Password-protected PDFs, print, and save a copy
  • Text recognition for scanned pages, on your device
  • Join, split, reorder and turn pages, or turn pictures into a PDF
  • Sync between your computers through a folder you choose
  • E-books too: EPUB, MOBI, FB2 and CBZ, with the same dark page and highlights
  • English and Arabic, from a small phone to a wide screen

Private because there is nothing on a server

Reader343 has no account and no cloud. The app is a page your browser keeps, and your books live in your browser's storage on your device.

Nothing leaves your deviceBooks, notes, highlights and reading history are stored locally. The only exception is looking up a book's details online, and that is off until you turn it on.
Yours to take awayOne backup file holds your whole library. Restore it on another device, or just keep it.
Open sourceThe code is on GitHub under the MIT license. Anyone can check what it does.
