Reader343 is installed

Pages turn dark, text turns light, and colors keep their hue. Photos stay as they are. Everything runs on this device.

  1. Open PDF links in dark mode

    Chrome asks once whether Reader343 may read the PDFs you open on websites. It only reads PDF files, and sends nothing anywhere.

    Allowed

  2. Optional: PDFs saved on your computer

    To open local files automatically, turn on Allow access to file URLs on the extension's settings page. You can always use the Open button or drag a file into the viewer instead.

  3. See it in action

    A sample page with colored text, a chart, highlights and a photo.

Tip: press D in the viewer to switch between smart dark and the original colors.
